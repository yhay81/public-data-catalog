# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique evidence and implementations, not people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect credentials, personal data, and sensitive information.
- Respect data providers, usage terms, rate limits, and affected communities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or personal data, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. For a sensitive report, contact the maintainer through a private channel available from the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/yhay81). Do not post personal or sensitive incident details in a public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately and promptly as practical, disclose information only when necessary, and avoid conflicts of interest when reviewing an incident.
